The mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell. It produces ATP through a process called cellular respiration. The process involves three main stages: glycolysis, the Krebs cycle, and the electron transport chain. Glycolysis occurs in the cytoplasm and breaks down glucose into pyruvate. The Krebs cycle occurs in the mitochondrial matrix and produces NADH and FADH2. The electron transport chain occurs in the inner mitochondrial membrane and produces the majority of ATP. Oxygen is required for cellular respiration, making it an aerobic process.
